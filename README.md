# KimBiz

> A modern business management system designed to help businesses manage their operations, finances, customers, inventory, and more from one platform.

---

## 📌 About KimBiz

KimBiz is a business management SaaS system being developed by **Kelvin Mukami**.

The goal is to create a professional, scalable platform that can be used by businesses to manage their day-to-day operations from one place.

KimBiz is being designed with flexibility in mind so that different businesses can subscribe to only the modules they need.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Many small and medium-sized businesses manage their operations using a combination of:

- Excel spreadsheets
- Paper records
- WhatsApp
- Separate accounting tools
- Separate inventory systems
- Manual customer records

This can make it difficult to keep track of the overall health of a business.

KimBiz aims to bring these important business operations into one centralized system.

# 💳 Subscription System

KimBiz is planned as a subscription-based SaaS platform.

Businesses will be able to subscribe for different periods such as:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Half-yearly
- Yearly

Longer subscriptions will provide better value to encourage businesses to choose longer plans.

The system will automatically calculate subscription expiry dates.

Businesses will receive notifications when their subscription is approaching expiration.

After expiration, businesses will receive a short grace period before access to the system is restricted.

Access will automatically be restored once the subscription is renewed.

---

# 🧩 Modular Business System

KimBiz will support businesses that only need specific features.

For example:

A business may subscribe to:

- Sales
- Customers
- Inventory

while another business may only subscribe to:

- Expenses
- Reports

Businesses will only see and access the modules included in their subscription.

# 👨‍💼 KimBiz Admin Panel

The system owner will have a separate administration panel.

The admin panel will eventually allow the system owner to:

- View registered businesses
- View business information
- Monitor subscriptions
- See subscriptions approaching expiry
- View expired subscriptions
- Activate or deactivate businesses
- Manage subscription plans
- Manage business access
- View support tickets
- Respond to businesses
- Monitor platform activity

One business being suspended will not affect other businesses using KimBiz.
